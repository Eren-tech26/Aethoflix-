How to use the cinema

Walk with WASD / arrows, or the virtual joystick (bottom-left). Drag anywhere else to look around.

Sit: walk up to any of the 10 burgundy recliners and press E or the SIT button. The camera glides into cinema view, focused on the big screen.

Stand: press E or STAND UP — you can walk away freely.

Raised row: climb the glowing amber steps in the middle to reach Row 2.

Exit: walk through the doorway on the rear-right wall (follow the EXIT sign) into the warm hallway.

Watch Party: open 🎟 Watch Party, create a room, share the code. The host searches a title (🔍 TMDB search is built in — no key needed) and taps a poster to play it for the whole room after a 5s countdown, on the 3D cinema screen itself, in sync. ★ NXSHA is the default server; tap another pill to switch. Sit down for the full effect.

Playing: sit down, then 👆 tap the big screen once if it doesn't auto-play (browsers block auto-play with sound). The SCREEN REMOTE in 🎟 Watch Party gives ⏯ pause/play, ±10s seek (YouTube, synced by host), ⛶ big controls and 🔄 reload . While seated, the floating 👆 Touch / 👁 Look switch decides: Touch sends taps to the video, Look lets you drag around. The 🛡 shield blocks popup ads (ON by default — and if a player refuses to load, it turns itself off automatically and retries). Hit 🔒 Lock mid-movie to freeze all touch; hold 2s to unlock.

🦁 Brave browser: for the smoothest watch party, open AethoFlix in Brave (free · brave.com). Its built-in Shields block popups and ads on every streaming server automatically — no extensions needed, and it never breaks players the way heavy blockers can.
